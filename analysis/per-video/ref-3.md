## 1. Overview
Ad for **the reference title** with the "save the king" rescue narrative. The King is trapped in a sealed glass chamber filling with water while gravel/rubble piles in. Player clears a match board at the bottom; each match drains water / progresses rescue. A **heart timer ring** counts down; it runs out and the creative deliberately ends in **FAIL**, then the branded end card with "Play Now!".

Arc: cinematic intro (snake chases king) → simulated gameplay (tap-match, water draining, gravel encroaching) → tension as timer depletes → FAIL → end card (6 vignettes + Play Now, looping).

## 3. Core Mechanic
**Tap-to-match (match-2/3 "blast" style).** A single pointing-finger cursor (red/white royal bracelet) taps tile clusters that burst (red/blue/gold confetti). Each clear progresses the rescue (drain water / lower threat). Gravel/rubble continuously falls and fills the chamber + board gaps (obstacle). A heart-ring countdown depletes over time; empty = FAIL.

## 4. UI
- **Heart timer / lives ring** (top-left): red heart in a circular ring; radial countdown green (full)→orange→red/empty.
- **Match board** (bottom ~40%): red rounded blocks ~#E0202A, blue shields ~#2C7BE0, gold crowns ~#F2C014 on cream/tan cells.
- **Hand cursor** (red/white striped cuff).
- **FAIL button:** red, white inner ring, gold rim, white "FAIL".
- **End card:** "[TITLE]" logo; 6 vignette cards (2×3); green "Play Now!" (blue glow outline). No score/coins/progress numerals in gameplay; only the heart ring.
